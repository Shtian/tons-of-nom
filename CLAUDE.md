## Design Context

Frontend/design work is governed by two root files — read them before designing or changing UI:

- **`PRODUCT.md`** — strategy: register (`product`), users, purpose, brand personality (dark/heavy/serious death-metal), anti-references, design principles, a11y bar.
- **`DESIGN.md`** — visual system: North Star "The Blackened Stage", crimson-on-stage-black palette, Geist + Faceless type, flat tonal-layered elevation, component specs and Do's/Don'ts. Machine-readable tokens live in its YAML frontmatter; `.impeccable/design.json` is the live-panel sidecar.

These were generated by `/impeccable init` + `document`. Re-run `/impeccable document` if the visual system drifts.
